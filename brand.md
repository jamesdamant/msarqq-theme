# Brand Identity — London Dog Leash Brand

Oct 25, 2026 · @James Damant

## Name and tagline

Recommended name: **Mews & Hound**. A mews is a cobbled London lane of old stables and coach houses, so the name carries heritage, craft and walking in one word. It reads as distinctly London in the UK and as charming and premium in the US.

**Tagline:** Designed in London. Made for the walk.

Alternative taglines:

- Every walk, a little more London.
- Proper leads for proper walks.
- From Mayfair to Manhattan.

Shortlist if Mews & Hound is taken:

| Name | Why it works | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mews & Hound (recommended) | Heritage, craft, short, easy to say in both markets | Check UK and US trademarks and the .com |
| Hackney & Hound | Nod to Hackney carriages and East London cool | Less polished feel in the US |
| Kensington Lead Co. | Instantly posh, very clear | "Lead" is UK-only; US buyers search "leash" |
| Brass & Bramble | Matches the rope-and-brass look | Less obviously London |
| Saville Row Dog | Tailoring story | Too close to Savile Row; legal risk |

Open question: before buying a domain, search the UK IPO, USPTO and Amazon for each name. Secure the .com, .co.uk and matching Instagram and TikTok handles together.

## Brand story and positioning

Mews & Hound makes understated, London-styled leads and collars for owners who treat the daily walk as part of how they dress. The look is quiet luxury: soft rope, natural leather, aged brass, muted heritage colours.

**Positioning statement:** For style-conscious dog owners in the UK and US, Mews & Hound is the London accessories label that makes the daily walk feel considered, because every piece is designed around London's heritage materials and its love of the walk.

**Brand story (website "Our Story" version):**

London is a city of walkers, and its dogs know it. From the cobbled mews of Kensington to the towpaths of Regent's Canal, the walk is where the city slows down.

Mews & Hound began with a simple frustration: dog leads were either loud and plastic or stiff and over-designed. We wanted something that looked like it belonged with a good wool coat and a pair of worn-in boots. So we made it.

Each piece pairs soft, hand-finished rope with solid brass hardware, in colours borrowed from the city: Portland stone, Thames grey, Hyde Park green, Chelsea blush. Designed in London, made to be walked in every day, anywhere.

**Brand pillars:**

1. **Quiet luxury.** Muted colours, no loud logos, materials that age well.
2. **London heritage.** Every colour and collection is named after a real corner of the city.
3. **Built for the daily walk.** Strong hardware, soft hand-feel, easy to clean.
4. **A matching set.** Lead, collar and poo-bag holder sold as a coordinated look.

**Price position:** premium but reachable. Suggested lead £35–£45 / $45–$55, collar £28–£35 / $35–$42, set £65 / $80. This sits above high-street brands and below designer labels.

## Target customers

The core buyer in both markets is a 25–45-year-old urban professional, mostly women, who owns a small-to-medium dog and posts the walk. The US buyer is buying into "London" as an aesthetic; the UK buyer is buying quality and a local brand.

| Persona | Market | Who they are | What they want | Where to reach them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The City Walker | UK | 28–40, lives in a London flat, cockapoo or dachshund, walks before work | Something that matches her coat, lasts, and feels local | Instagram, TikTok, local pet boutiques, park pop-ups |
| The Anglophile | US | 28–45, New York, Boston, Chicago or LA, French bulldog or doodle | A bit of London in her daily routine; loves British style | Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, Etsy, Amazon |
| The Gifter | UK and US | Any age, buying for a friend's new puppy or for Christmas | A beautiful box and a matching set | Gift guides, Google Shopping, Etsy |
| The Country Weekender | UK | 35–55, London on weekdays, Cotswolds at weekends, Labrador or spaniel | Tough, smart kit for muddy walks and the pub | Instagram, country shows, Facebook |

Lead with The City Walker and The Anglophile; they share the same aesthetic, so one set of photos and copy serves both.

## Visual identity

The look is a heritage London shopfront: warm neutrals, deep racing green, brass accents, serif type and lots of white space. Your rope-and-brass sample sets the tone.

**Colour palette:**

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Mews Green | #1F3A2E | Logo, headers, buttons |
| Accent | Brass | #B08D57 | Lines, icons, hover states, foil on packaging |
| Background | Portland Stone | #F4EFE6 | Website background, tissue paper |
| Soft accent | Chelsea Blush | #D9B8A8 | Highlights, sale tags, the blush product line |
| Text | Soot | #2A2A2A | Body text |

**Typography:**

- Headings: a classic serif such as Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond (free on Google Fonts).
- Body: a clean sans-serif such as Inter or Lato.
- Wordmark: "MEWS & HOUND" in spaced capitals, with "LONDON" small underneath.

**Logo idea:** a simple line-drawn hound's head inside an arched mews doorway, in brass on green. Keep a text-only version for small sizes.

**Photography style:**

- Real London backdrops: cobbled mews, painted front doors, Georgian railings, park benches, autumn leaves.
- Close-ups of hands, rope texture and brass clips in soft natural light.
- Owners shown from the knee down or from behind, wearing trench coats, wool and boots.
- Avoid studio white backgrounds except for product listing shots.

**Packaging:**

- Green kraft box with a brass-foil logo, Portland Stone tissue paper and a brass sticker seal.
- A small card inside: "Walked in London. Now yours." plus a QR code to the care guide.
- Use recyclable materials and say so on the box; both markets reward it.

## Voice and tone

The voice is a well-dressed Londoner: warm, dry, a little witty, never shouty. Think a friendly shopkeeper on a Chelsea side street.

- **Do:** short sentences, gentle British humour ("a proper walk", "rain or shine, mostly rain"), real place names, concrete details about materials.
- **Don't:** exclamation marks everywhere, "fur babies", heavy slang, discount-shouting.

**UK vs US adaptation:**

|  | UK site | US site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main product word | Lead | Leash (use "lead" sparingly as British charm) |
| Spelling | Colour, personalised | Color, personalized |
| Units | cm first, inches in brackets | Inches first, cm in brackets |
| Currency | £, VAT included | $, sales tax at checkout |
| Delivery word | Delivery | Shipping |
| Selling angle | Quality and a local London brand | The London lifestyle, imported |
| Key gifting dates | Christmas, Mother's Day (March) | Christmas, Black Friday, Mother's Day (May), National Dog Day (26 Aug) |

Shopify Markets (or similar) lets you run both from one store with local currency, spelling and pricing.

## Launch checklist

Aim to be live before Christmas gifting peaks in late November.

- [ ] Claim Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest handles
- [ ] Commission the logo and wordmark from the visual identity brief
- [ ] Confirm supplier, materials, minimum order and unit cost; order samples in all four colours
- [ ] Check dog product safety and labelling rules for the UK and US
- [ ] Shoot photos on location in a London mews, plus white-background listing shots
- [ ] Build the Shopify store with Shopify Markets for GBP and USD
- [ ] Set up shipping: a UK carrier plus a US route (direct or a US fulfilment warehouse)
- [ ] Order branded boxes, tissue and insert cards
- [ ] Launch the email signup and a 10% welcome offer
- [ ] Send sets to 10–20 London and New York dog influencers
